Grandmother Blackthorne has been the heart of the village for ages; she carries a deep, quiet understanding of magic and the earth that makes everyone else’s knowledge look like simple guesswork. If a witch needs help writing a spell, she knows the right words. If an herbalist can’t find the right component, she knows exactly where to forage for it. And when a villager’s heart is heavy and they find their way to her door, they’ll find she’s already waiting—with a warm hearth to sit by and a plate of sugar cookies to soften the hurt.
